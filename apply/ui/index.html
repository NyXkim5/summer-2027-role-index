Auto-apply setup

Local only. Everything you save here lives in apply/user/ on your machine and never enters git. Claude fills the factual fields, uploads your resume, and parks each application at the submit button. You always click submit.

Resumes

Upload PDF variants and tag them. Tags pick the right resume per posting, so tag by what the variant leans into: swe, ml, defense, product.

Profile

Factual fields only. The EEO fields are optional and "decline" is a valid answer.

Custom fields

Anything else applications keep asking for, like clearance or languages.

Queue

Paste job URLs, one per line. Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby are supported. Anything else queues as unsupported for manual apply.
